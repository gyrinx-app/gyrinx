EXACT COVERAGE (checked against Django 6.0.7's own form templates, not assumed). Only django/forms/div.html routes fields through this file, via `field.as_field_group`: * `{{ form }}` -> as_div -> div.html -> HERE (27 sites) * `{{ form.as_div }}` -> div.html -> HERE (1 site) * `{{ field.as_field_group }}` -> HERE (2 sites) * `{{ form.as_p }}` -> p.html -> does NOT reach this file. p.html inlines `{{ field.label_tag }}` / `{{ field }}` / `` itself. The three as_p sites are admin/gyrinxsite/notification/broadcast.html (Django-admin CSS) and two vendored allauth MFA templates — both surfaces the migration leaves alone by design. They keep Django's stock rendering. test_cotton_call_site_gates.py pins that list so it cannot grow. So this one line moves 30 sites onto the component with ZERO edits to any of those templates, and it is still the highest-leverage edit in the form work. It also ends a real split. This file used to be a three-branch cascade that DISAGREED with core/includes/form_field.html on both help text (`div.helptext.form-text` vs `small.form-text.text-secondary`) and errors (bare errorlist vs `div.invalid-feedback.d-block`). The app shipped two different-looking field renderings depending on whether a template said `{{ form }}` or included the partial. Now there is one.
