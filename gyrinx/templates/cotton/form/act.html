Why a form and not a link: these change server state, so they must not be reachable by GET (a prefetching browser or a crawler would fire them). This component exists so that fact is structural rather than remembered — there is no `method` prop, and the CSRF token cannot be omitted. Props action target URL. Defaults to `d-inline` (28 of the 32 sites); override for `d-inline ms-2`, `m-0`, etc. Default slot: the button, and any hidden inputs. Icon-only buttons MUST carry an aria-label — that is the caller's job, on , and it is worth a test-time assertion rather than a runtime one. NO JavaScript: no onsubmit confirm, no fetch.
