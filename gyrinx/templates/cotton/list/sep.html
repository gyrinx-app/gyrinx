{% comment %} c-list.sep — the separator between two c-list items in slot mode. Renders , . The non-breaking space is the point: it keeps the comma welded to the item before it so a long list never wraps with a leading comma on the next line. One dense line, no trailing newline -- it sits between two items inside {% spaceless %} and any whitespace it emitted would defeat the wrapper. {% endcomment %},
