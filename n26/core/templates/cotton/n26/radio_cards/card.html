{% comment %} — one option in a grid. 6 entries A sibling of rather than a mode of it, because the two differ where it counts. A checkbox card's body goes dim and inert while the card is unticked — choices inside an unselected card are choices about something that is not happening. In a group where exactly one thing is picked, every card but one is unpicked at all times, so the same treatment would grey out every option the reader is trying to compare and make the group read as disabled. This card has no dependent body and nothing goes dim: an option not yet chosen is not off. It also cannot own its state the way the checkbox card does. One-of-many is the browser's rule over a shared `name`, not something a card can decide about itself — which is the other half of why this is its own component, and why there is no JavaScript here at all. The whole card is the label, so a press anywhere on it selects; the radio stays visible, because a group of cards with no controls in them does not look like a question. name — shared across every card in the group.
