{% comment %} — a list of things to choose and unchoose one at a time. Nothing is on offer here yet. Takes the same n26.render.ChoiceOffer that draws, and is a sibling of it rather than a mode, for the reason radio cards and checkbox cards are siblings: one of many is the browser's rule over a shared name, and a choice that holds three picks is not that question. There is no selection to keep here and nothing to save at the end — each option carries its own act, and a click settles or unsettles that one option and nothing else. The offer says which act each option gets (n26.render.Choosable.control): what the choice already holds is taken back, what it does not is added, and when the choice is full the rest are not listed at all — the way to something else is to take a pick back, never to have one pushed out unasked. Buttons and no script. It draws submits and no form of its own, so it goes inside one — the page it sits on owns the action and the token, and only the button that was clicked is sent, which is how the two acts tell themselves apart. Rows rather than cards: the option's name and a line about it read down a column, and the act sits at the end of its own row where the eye finishes it. :offer — a n26.render.ChoiceOffer. name — the input name the add act submits under, matching what the view reads. Taking one back always submits under "remove". empty — what to say when there is nothing to pick. An empty offer is a thing to say rather than a page to hide: what to say depends on why it is empty, which the page knows and this does not. {% endcomment %} {% comment %} space-y-8 between groups, the same rhythm keeps, so a choice that holds several does not read as a different application from one that holds one. {% endcomment %}
