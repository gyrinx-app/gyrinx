{% comment %} — a selectable card whose body stays interactive. 85¢ …rows of dependent controls… Use this instead of the kit's checkbox cards (c-ui.checkbox.group variant="cards") when the card holds controls of its own: the kit makes the whole surface the toggle, so a click on any of them would tick the card. Here the toggle is confined to the header row and the body stays live. The presentation matches the kit's, so the two read as one family. While unticked the body is dimmed and inert. `inert` stops interaction and focus but not form submission — an input that must not submit while the card is unticked binds :disabled="!picked" itself. `picked` is in Alpine scope for the body and meta slots to read. name, value — the form identity of the card's own checkbox. :checked — the starting state. label — the card's title. description — the muted line under it. Optional. meta — a slot at the header's far end: a price, a counter. Optional. slot — the body, under a rule. Optional; without one the card is just its header. {% endcomment %}
