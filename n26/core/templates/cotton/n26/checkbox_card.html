{% comment %} — a selectable card whose body stays interactive. 85¢ …rows of dependent controls… The kit's own checkbox cards (c-ui.checkbox.group variant="cards") make the whole surface the toggle, which is right up until the card holds controls of its own — then a press on any of them would toggle the card. This one confines the toggle to its header row and keeps the body live, which is the difference that justifies a second component. The presentation is deliberately the kit's: border-2 to the accent with a light tint while ticked, so the two read as one family whichever a screen uses. While unticked the body is dead — dimmed and inert, because choices inside an unselected card are choices about something that is not happening. inert stops interaction and focus but not form submission: an input inside that must not submit while the card is unticked binds :disabled="!picked" itself. `picked` is in Alpine scope for the body and meta slots to read. name, value — the form identity of the card's own checkbox. :checked — the starting state. label — the card's title. description — the muted line under it. Optional. meta — a slot at the header's far end: a price, a counter. Optional. slot — the body, under a rule. Optional; without one the card is just its header. {% endcomment %}
