A part offers no move. It belongs to the thing it hangs off — ammo is ammo *for* a gun — and Operation.move refuses a firing line, so the structure gives it none to draw. An accessory is the case that could be moved, but the way to re-fit one is from wherever it is being kept rather than from the gun it is already on.
