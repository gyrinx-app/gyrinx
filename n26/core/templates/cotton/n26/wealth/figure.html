{% comment %} — one labelled figure in the wealth strip. The short name over the figure, both centred, in a column of their own. Authored once: the term and its value are the same pair, so nothing here can fall out of step with the row above or below it. The full name arrives through c-ui.tooltip rather than a title attribute, which is where this parts company with . That component's reasoning is about volume — a card carries a dozen columns and a gang sheet hundreds, so it spends nothing per cell. There are four of these on a page, so they can afford the thing that actually works on a phone: a title never appears at all without a mouse, and "R" is not a word. short — the abbreviation shown. Two characters at most; Cr, not Cred. full — what it stands for, revealed on hover or tap. :value — credits, as an integer. The ¢ is drawn here so a caller never has to remember which figures carry it. unit — what the figure is denominated in: ¢ unless said otherwise. A figure that counts things rather than money passes an empty one. unset — why this figure has no answer, as a sentence. Given one, the cell draws a muted em dash carrying it and ignores `value`. For a figure that is not zero but absent: a gang with no budget has not spent nothing, it has nothing to spend against, and a 0¢ there would be read as bankrupt. {% endcomment %}
