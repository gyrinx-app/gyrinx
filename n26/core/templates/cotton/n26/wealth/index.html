{% comment %} — what the gang is worth, as a figure strip. Four figures, in the order they answer questions about each other: rating is what the gang fields, credits what it has left, stash what it owns and nobody is carrying, and wealth the three added together. Reading left to right is reading the sum being made, which is why this is a strip and not four badges — badges would put the numbers in no relation at all. Takes the whole GangSheet rather than four numbers, and that is the point: four positional integers of the same type in the same units are four chances to swap two of them and never find out. GangSheet already holds them under names. --- why a dl and not a table --- The statline next door is a two-row table, and this looks like one. It is not: a table needs its headers and its values in separate rows, so the labels and the figures would have to be written out twice and kept in step by hand. A definition list says the same thing — four terms, four values — with each pair authored once, and lays out as columns with flex. The type scale, padding and tabular figures are mirrored from on purpose, and a test holds them to it, because the two sit inches apart on a gang sheet and reading as one system is the whole job. :sheet — a n26.render.GangSheet. class — passed to the scroll container. Where a page wants the strip fenced off, `border-l border-box-border pl-3` is the rule it used to draw itself. {% endcomment %} {% comment %} No rule down the left. Whether this strip needs fencing off from what sits beside it depends entirely on what that is — beside a gang's name it helps, repeated down a list of rows it becomes five short lines that look like a table someone forgot to finish. That is a layout question, so it belongs to the layout: pass class="border-l border-box-border pl-3" where the page wants one. Scrolls rather than wraps, for the same reason the statline does: a strip whose last figure drops onto a second row stops reading as a strip, and "wealth" is the one a reader is most often after. {% endcomment %}
