{% comment %} — Trade Points, then rating, credits, stash and wealth for a gang. Trade Points are not money and sit outside the sum. They carry no credits sign. Rating is what the models are worth. Stashed gear counts in wealth instead. Wealth is rating plus credits plus stash rating, in that order. A zero is a real figure. Only the unset message draws a dash, and it ignores the value. A shut trading post and unlimited credits pass unset so they draw a dash. A remaining zero does not. Pass the whole sheet, not loose integers. Two figures in the same units are easy to swap and hard to notice. {% endcomment %}
