{% comment %} — what the gang is worth, as a figure strip. Four money figures, left to right in the order that makes the sum: rating is what the gang fields, credits what it has left to spend, stash what it owns and nobody is carrying, and wealth the three added together. Trade Points lead, behind a rule. They are not money and not part of the sum, being what an open Visit Trading Post action has left, so they must not sit in a run of figures a reader adds up. They carry no unit. With no action open the cell draws an em dash: the post is shut to the gang, which is not the same as a visit that has spent everything. It takes the whole sheet rather than loose integers, because positional numbers of the same type in the same units are that many chances to swap two and never find out. A definition list, not the two-row table it resembles: a table would need the labels and the figures in separate rows, written out twice and kept in step by hand.
