The count is the control: a button reading the number of models the gang fields, which opens two readings of that same roster — how many of each profile at each rank, and what each model is worth. The figure a reader glances at and the breakdown they occasionally check are one thing, so there is one place to look and one thing to click. Both tables keep the roster's own order, pets after their keepers, so the tally reads down the way the sheet does. The counts total matches the number on the trigger; the ratings total need not match the gang's rating figure, since a gang can carry worth that no single model does. The number carries no unit, unlike every figure in beside it: it counts models, not credits. The icon says which, and the tooltip says it in words — a drawing is no more a word than "M" was. :summary — a n26.render.RosterSummary.
