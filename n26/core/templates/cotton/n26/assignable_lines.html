A computed line was granted rather than bought, and will appear and vanish as whatever grants it changes. It is marked with a dotted underline whose tooltip says where it came from, the same mark a modified characteristic gets in . Not a badge: granted skills are ordinary, and badging each one would make the common case look like an exception.
