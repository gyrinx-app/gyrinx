{% comment %} — how a form ends: the way out, then the act. draws its footer with this, so a full-page form gets one by filling in props; anything that is not a form-page — a dialog, or one of several forms on a page — writes the tag itself. The order and the alignment are not props: the way out first, the act last, right-aligned. Cancel is a plain href rather than a submit or a scripted step back through history, so a reader who clicks it lands where they already were. A form offering two acts puts the second in the slot with a name and value of its own. Only the clicked submit is sent, which is how the view tells the two apart, and the act at the end needs no name because its click sends nothing extra. submit_label — the act's wording. Without one no submit is drawn. submit_variant — the act's colour. `success` by default, because the button ending a form usually brings a thing into existence; `danger` where it takes one away, `primary` where it only carries on. cancel_url — where the way out leads. Without one there is no cancel, which is better than one leading somewhere arbitrary. size — both buttons' size, for a footer that ends a step rather than a screen. {% endcomment %} {% comment %} Wrapping rather than shrinking: two buttons that will not fit on one line drop onto two, still right-aligned, rather than squeezing their wording to nothing. {% endcomment %}
