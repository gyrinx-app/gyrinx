{% comment %} — one model's name and rank, and the tabs of their screens. …quick-switcher… …breadcrumbs… The one header every per-model screen draws. Edit and Equip are two faces of the same model, so they share a header and read as tabs of one place — a component rather than a convention, because two screens each writing "the same" header are two screens free to drift apart about what a model's page looks like. The title is the model's name plain, not "Equip Vesna": the tab strip says which face is open, and a title that repeated the verb would say it twice while making the name harder to scan for. The lead is the model's rank — what a reader checking "which of my models is this" wants said once at the top. The tab strip is built by the model_screen_tabs tag rather than passed in, so a screen cannot invent a strip of its own; adding a screen to a model is one edit to n26.core.navigation. :miniature — the model. Needs ``pk`` and ``name``; nothing else is read. lead — the model's rank, under the name. Empty draws nothing. active — which tab this screen is: ``edit`` or ``equip``. switcher — a control after the name, for the gang's other models: the pages pass pointed at this same screen for each of them. breadcrumb — the trail above the name. The pages build it, because who owns the gang and where its sheet lives are their business. actions — the header's far end, opposite the name: the gang's figures and the roster tally, the numbers a per-model decision is made against. The same corner the gang sheet keeps its wealth strip in, so a reader's eye finds the money in one place on every screen. {% endcomment %} {% load navigation %}
