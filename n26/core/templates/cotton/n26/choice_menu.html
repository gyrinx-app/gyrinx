{% comment %} — single choice in a dropdown. The sibling of , for the things that are one-of rather than many-of. Sorting is the obvious case: it is never several orders at once, so the multi-select panel is the wrong shape for it. Radios instead of checkboxes, and All / None and the per-row `only` go with them — they only mean something when more than one row can be on. What is left is the list, and OK / Cancel. Like its sibling it adds no state of its own beyond a snapshot. The radio group's Alpine scope holds the choice in a plain, assignable `value`, and the dropdown exposes `close()`; both sit above this content in the scope chain. The group wraps the whole dropdown so the trigger can show the current choice while shut. For a plain menu that commits the moment you pick — no OK, no Cancel — the kit's own is a better fit than this. label — trigger text. name — submitted field name; also the `name` on the applied event. :options — [{value, label}]. :selected — the initially chosen value. placeholder — shown in the trigger when nothing is chosen. apply_label / cancel_label — footer button text. align, position, min_width — passed through to the dropdown. Applying dispatches `choice-apply` with {name, value}; cancelling restores the choice as it was when the panel opened and dispatches nothing. {% endcomment %}
