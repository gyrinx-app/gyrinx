{% comment %} — a strip of tabs that are links, one of them current. Each tab is an , so the choice is a URL: linkable, in the history, and made without JavaScript. Behind a tab here is a whole render, which is what separates this from the kit's c-ui.tabs — panels already on the page, switched in the hand. It is also why a tab carries no count: only the chosen tab's contents have been built, and numbering the rest would mean building all of them on every load. Counting belongs inside whatever the chosen tab drew. A nav rather than role="tablist", because these navigate; a reader told "tab" by a screen reader expects arrow keys and a panel swapping underneath. aria-current="page" says which one they are on. The strip wraps rather than scrolling sideways, since tab names come from content and can be long, and a name too long for the width truncates rather than pushing the strip wider than the page. The rule under the strip is the tabs' own bottom borders, which the row makes one height so they meet as a continuous line; the trailing span carries it across the width the tabs do not fill. :tabs — [{label, href, current, title}]. `title` is optional and is the full name when `label` has been shortened to fit; it becomes the link's tooltip. label — what the strip is choosing between, as its accessible name. speculate — "prefetch" or "prerender", asking the browser to start on the other tabs before they are clicked. Prefetch holds the HTML only and the click still parses and boots the page; prerender builds the whole hidden page for an instant click but keeps a full document per tab in memory, so reach for it only where the pages are light. Off by default. Browsers with no speculation rules ignore it and the tabs stay ordinary links. Every linked page must be a side-effect-free GET, since the browser may fetch it before anyone chooses it. eagerness — when to start. "moderate" (the default) waits for hover or touch-down; "immediate" starts as soon as the page settles, buying the most head start on pages that may never be opened. {% endcomment %}
