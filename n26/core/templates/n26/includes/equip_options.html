Not lines of their own, because that is not what they are: a profile belongs to one particular weapon, so buying one is a way of buying that weapon rather than a second purchase from the list. Same shape as profile-picker.row's option groups, and read back the same way — indices into the row the server re-derives. No heading over the boxes. Ticked rounds under a gun, priced and named, say what they are — and a label saying so would print on every armed row of a long list to tell a reader something the row beneath it already told them. Each carries a price box of its own, in the column the gun's box is in: they are separate charges on one press, and a discount on the gun is not a discount on the rounds. Lining them up is what says they are the same kind of number. The rows have no Buy — they ride the gun's — so what stands where the button would be is an invisible copy of it, which keeps the columns together whatever the button is later made to say. {% endcomment %} {% for option in row.options %} {% comment %} The box and the name are one label, so the hit target is most of the row rather than a control the width of a fingertip — this list is read on a phone.
