Print lab

The <c-n26.print.*> components against real paper geometry. Printing is the one part of the system you cannot check by looking at it, and its worst failures — a card sliced across the fold, a two-column block collapsed to one — happen on engines other than the one you are in. So: change something, look at the preview, then print it to PDF and look at that too.

{% comment %} An iframe, not an include. The sheet has to be its own document for the same reason it has its own URL: @page is document-level, and a sheet rendered inside the gallery would be pinned to the gallery's page rather than to its own. The preview is the artefact, loaded the way a printer would load it. {% endcomment %} {# djlint:off H021 — print column widths live on the cells; print engines read them there #}

The preview renders the sheet at its true physical size, so what you see is the artefact rather than an impression of it. On a narrow screen it scrolls sideways instead of shrinking, deliberately.
