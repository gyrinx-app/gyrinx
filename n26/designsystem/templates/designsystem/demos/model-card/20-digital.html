{# title: The awkward cases #} {# note: Four things worth looking at. The Grenade launcher has two named profiles that are both free, so its stats cannot be hoisted onto its name row — frag is not the weapon. The Lasgun's second row is ammo bought later, priced separately and totalling onto the weapon. Unstable is marked because it was granted rather than printed, and so are the skills a Leader brings with them. WS is marked for the same reason — it is not the printed value. Hover any of them. #} {# layout: full #}
