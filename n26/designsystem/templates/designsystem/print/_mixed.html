{% comment %} The mixed specimen: a title block, cards, a forced page break, then the table. Two things worth having a specimen for. One is doing its job — the reference table starting on a fresh sheet rather than sharing the bottom of the last page of cards, which is tidier on paper and worse to use. The other is content that is not in the grid at all. A sheet is an ordinary block flow: entries, headings and tables can sit straight on it, and only the things that must not be cut need to be cards. Getting that wrong in the other direction — wrapping everything in cards so nothing ever breaks — is how a document ends up with a page and a half of white space in it. {% endcomment %}
