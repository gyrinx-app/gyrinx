{% load react %} {% comment %} The two-step composer, shared by every carrier page and the standalone create page. Step one is a GET so the chosen kinds live in the URL — step two survives a refresh, and "add a condition" is a plain link. Takes modifier_kind_picker, composer, composer_scope, composer_effect, composer_chips, and modifier_naming. --- where step one leads --- `composer_action` is where step one goes, and a caller that leaves it out is answered by the page it is already on. A carrier's page sends it to the composer's own address instead, naming itself in `composer_carrier_kind` and `composer_carrier_pk`: choosing the two kinds redraws whatever page is answering, and a carrier's page is long, so the reader is put back at its top and has to scroll down to the form they were filling in — every time they change either kind. The carrier rides both forms as hidden inputs. On the GET they become the query string, so the composing page is a real address that survives a refresh and can be linked to; on the POST they say what to attach to and where to go back to. --- the reusable switch --- `modifier_naming` is offered wherever there is a carrier to name the modifier after, which is the whole of what the switch decides. The standalone page keeps the ordinary name field and no switch: with nothing to attach to, everything it makes is reusable and there is no choice to put in front of anyone. --- what the caller owns --- The heading, the box, the submit's wording and where the way out leads belong to the caller: on a carrier's page this is one group among several and needs both to read as a group, with nowhere for a cancel to lead that is not the page it is already on, while on its own page the header has already said what it is, a box around the whole page would enclose nothing, and leaving means going back to the carrier that sent you — or to the listing, reached with none. There are no defaults — a caller that leaves one out gets a section with no heading. {% endcomment %} {% comment %} Step one, as a GET: the chosen kinds live in the URL, so step two survives a refresh and "add a condition" can be a plain link.
